# Medical Specialist Mapping

Use this reference to determine which specialists are required and who should be designated as the lead, based on the clinical content of the document.

| Specialty | Key Indicators / Keywords | Common Tasks | Lead Potential | Lead Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cardiologist** | ECG, EKG, heart failure, arrhythmia, chest pain, troponin, echocardiogram, hypertension. | Review cardiac function, rhythm analysis, and blood pressure management. | High | Primary issue is cardiac-related (e.g., acute MI, severe heart failure). |
| **Neurologist** | Stroke, seizure, neuropathy, MRI Brain, CT Head, syncope, tremors, cognitive decline. | Assess neurological deficits, imaging of the CNS, and seizure management. | High | Primary issue is neurological (e.g., acute stroke, new-onset seizures). |
| **Pulmonologist** | COPD, asthma, pneumonia, SOB (shortness of breath), PFTs, CXR, pulse oximetry, intubation. | Evaluate lung function, respiratory support, and airway management. | High | Primary issue is respiratory (e.g., severe pneumonia, acute exacerbation of COPD). |
| **Gastroenterologist** | GI bleed, endoscopy, colonoscopy, cirrhosis, abdominal pain, LFTs, jaundice, nausea. | Review digestive tract issues, liver function, and endoscopic findings. | Medium | Primary issue is gastrointestinal, but often co-managed. |
| **Nephrologist** | AKI, CKD, creatinine, dialysis, electrolyte imbalance, proteinuria, hematuria. | Manage kidney function, fluid balance, and electrolyte correction. | Medium | Primary issue is renal, but often secondary to other conditions. |
| **Endocrinologist** | Diabetes, HbA1c, thyroid, adrenal, pituitary, hypoglycemia, hyperglycemia. | Optimize glucose control and hormonal balance. | Medium | Primary issue is endocrine, but often co-managed with other specialties. |
| **Infectious Disease** | Fever, sepsis, cultures, antibiotics, HIV, MRSA, leukocytosis, procalcitonin. | Select appropriate antimicrobial therapy and manage complex infections. | High | Primary issue is a severe or complex infection (e.g., sepsis, endocarditis). |
| **Oncologist** | Malignancy, biopsy, chemotherapy, radiation, staging, PET scan, tumor markers. | Review cancer staging, treatment protocols, and palliative care. | High | Primary issue is cancer diagnosis or management. |
| **Radiologist** | Imaging interpretation, X-ray, CT, MRI, Ultrasound, PET, Interventional. | Provide detailed interpretation of diagnostic imaging. | Low | Rarely the lead, typically provides supporting interpretation. |
| **Pharmacist** | Medication reconciliation, drug interactions, dosing, polypharmacy, contraindications. | Optimize drug therapy and check for safety/interactions. | Low | Essential support, but rarely the lead. |
| **Surgeon (General)** | Acute abdomen, trauma, pre-op, post-op, hernia, appendicitis, cholecystitis. | Evaluate surgical necessity and post-operative recovery. | High | Primary issue requires surgical intervention. |
