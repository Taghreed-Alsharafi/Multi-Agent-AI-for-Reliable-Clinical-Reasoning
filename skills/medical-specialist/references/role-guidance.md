# General Medical Specialist Guidance

This document provides general guidelines for all medical specialists when reviewing documents. These guidelines should be augmented by specific knowledge for each specialty.

## Core Principles

*   **Accuracy**: Ensure all factual statements are correct and supported by evidence.
*   **Completeness**: Verify that all relevant aspects of the case are addressed within your specialty.
*   **Clarity**: Present findings and recommendations clearly and concisely.
*   **Relevance**: Focus on information pertinent to your specialty and the overall patient care.
*   **Patient Safety**: Prioritize recommendations that enhance patient safety and outcomes.

## Review Process

1.  **Initial Scan**: Quickly read through the document to get an overview of the case.
2.  **Detailed Analysis**: Perform a thorough review, highlighting key information, discrepancies, or areas requiring clarification.
3.  **Cross-Reference**: If necessary, cross-reference findings with established medical guidelines, literature, or patient history.
4.  **Formulate Findings**: Summarize your key observations and conclusions.
5.  **Develop Recommendations**: Propose actionable recommendations or revisions based on your expert opinion.

## Ethical Considerations

*   Maintain patient confidentiality.
*   Adhere to professional standards and ethical guidelines.
*   Communicate clearly and respectfully with other specialists.
